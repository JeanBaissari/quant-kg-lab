## Workflow playbooks (composable stack)

- [`quant-factor-importance`](../skills/quant-patterns/factor-importance/SKILL.md) — Use when ranking features by predictive power in trading models — permutation importance, SHAP values, MDI (mean decrease in impurity), and factor decay analysis. Integrates scikit-learn inspection and ensemble modules.
- [`quant-factor-research`](../skills/quant-patterns/factor-research/SKILL.md) — Use when researching alpha factors — building candidate features, measuring their predictive power and importance, selecting a robust subset, and validating out-of-sample before any backtest.
- [`quant-full-pipeline`](../skills/quant-patterns/full-pipeline/SKILL.md) — Use when building an end-to-end quantitative research loop — from raw OHLCV data through features, model, backtest, hyperparameter search, and risk analysis — composing the whole quant stack.
- [`quant-hpo-optimization`](../skills/quant-patterns/hpo-optimization/SKILL.md) — Use when tuning trading strategy hyperparameters via Bayesian optimization — Optuna study lifecycle, sampler selection, pruning strategies, and distributed parallel sweeps for walk-forward backtesting.
- [`quant-ml-strategy`](../skills/quant-patterns/ml-strategy/SKILL.md) — Use when turning a machine-learning model into a tradable strategy — mapping predictions to positions, backtesting on an execution engine, tuning with HPO, and sizing with risk controls.
- [`quant-portfolio-construction`](../skills/quant-patterns/portfolio-construction/SKILL.md) — Use when building portfolios from strategy signals — mean-variance optimization, risk parity, Black-Litterman, and position sizing from Kelly criterion. Integrates scikit-learn covariance and decomposition for risk modeling.
- [`quant-regime-detection`](../skills/quant-patterns/regime-detection/SKILL.md) — Use when detecting market regimes for conditional strategy switching — Hidden Markov Models, Gaussian Mixture Models, threshold-based segmentation, and volatility clustering. Integrates scikit-learn mixture, cluster, and decomposition modules.
- [`quant-walk-forward-validation`](../skills/quant-patterns/walk-forward-validation/SKILL.md) — Use when implementing walk-forward validation, time-series cross-validation, or rolling/expanding window backtesting for trading strategies. Integrates scikit-learn TimeSeriesSplit with custom purging/embargo logic.
